    Missing pieces are switched off; everything else still works.

    Glossary

    Ask about development economics anywhere, or economic opportunity in the US. Every number comes from a live data source and is fact-checked; each answer shows what was looked up and lets you download the data.

    Make a report

    Python fetches every number and writes every table; the model only writes a short summary (one request, fact-checked). Download it as PDF, Word, Markdown, LaTeX, or the data itself.

    Topics

    Income, growth (with long-run history and the World Bank's forecast), poverty, health and education against the country's income group and region; differences between regions; J-PAL evaluations and library papers about the country.

    The $3.00, $4.20 and $8.30 poverty lines for every survey year, the poverty gap, the number of poor, how old and how far apart the surveys are, peers, and income by region.

    Randomized evaluations from J-PAL grouped by outcome and region (their results quoted, not judged), reviews and meta-analyses from OpenAlex, and passages from your library. Every citation in the summary is checked against them.
